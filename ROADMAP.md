# AEN Roadmap — Draft/Pilot

This roadmap separates implemented engineering from validated product outcomes. Dates are not promises, and no phase may claim a higher maturity level without its published evidence gate.

## Current: AEXP 0.1 Draft / reference implementation

- M0 protocol, schemas, digest/signature, and conformance: implemented.
- M1 DSH offline adapter, native Cordis plugin, live Manifest, and local store: implemented with DSH 0.1.0-rc.7 composition tests.
- M2 private Experience loop: implemented.
- M3 comparative evaluation engine: implemented; the real 2 Model × 2 Manifest × 2 task-family pilot is pending credentials, budget, tasks, and live runs.
- M4 Promotion and single Reference Hub core: implemented with a portable out-of-workspace deployment and container starting point; a reviewed public deployment and independent two-developer transcript are pending.
- M5 compatibility-first consumption and repair loop: implemented.
- M6 documentation, sample adapter, and pre-public engineering audit: implemented; independent contributor usability evidence and the real pilot report remain pending.

## Next pilot gates

1. Freeze a reviewed AEXP 0.1 Draft release candidate and publish migration notes.
2. Recruit at least three developers who do not share a local evidence database.
3. Preregister two task families, two ModelFingerprints, two DSH HarnessManifest configurations, primary metrics, repeats, stopping rules, privacy limits, and budget.
4. Run baseline/treatment cells with real graders; publish all eligible failures and uncertainty.
5. Complete cross-user publish → discover → immutable section read → inject → feedback → measured observation transcripts.
6. Exercise emergency secret and license revocation on a disposable public fixture.
7. Obtain an independent security review and an independent adapter/contribution usability report.

## Later, only after demand and exit criteria

- richer search and artifact/reproduction profiles;
- team/enterprise policy domains;
- additional production Harness adapters;
- federation only after multiple independent operators provide real interoperability and failure-model requirements;
- Stable only after at least two independent compatible implementations, migrations, production adopters, a published security process, and the AEXP maturity gate.

Marketplace, global ranking, token incentives, federation, and automatic remote execution are not hidden MVP work. Proposals for them must preserve AEXP object portability, user choice, negative evidence, and local-only operation.
